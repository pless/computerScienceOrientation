Today in lab:
  We are going to make a "persistent web app" on your phone.  This acts like an app on your phone, but doesn't require you to register through the App Store and works automatically on both Android and iPhone.  here are the instructions:
1. Paste the link to your github repository from last class into the AI of your choice, and ask it to generate the files nessesary to turn the web app there into a persistent web app.
2. Download the files it creates. These should include a manifest file, a serviceworker.js or sw.js file, and icons for your app. It will also regenerate your index.html file to accomodate the changes needed.  
3. Go back to the main page of your repository, then click the plus button immediate to the left of the green code button. Select upload files in the dropdown that comes up, and upload all the files other than the new index.html
4. Open the new index.html file, and hit Control-A Control-C to copy the contents. 
5. Go back to the main page of your repository, and click on the index.html link. 
6. In the upper right click on the pencil icon, then once you are in the edit window, click on the contents of the file and hit Control-A, and then delete. Then hit Control-V to paste the new index.html into the box. Click the green commit changes button in the upper right, then click the commit changes button in the window that pops up. 
7. Give it about a minute or two to build all these changes, then open the [your-username].github.io url on your phone, hit the share button for the page (it should look something like a small box with an arrow sticking out of it) and scroll through the options until you see "add to home screen". Click on that and make sure that "Open as a Web App" is set to yes if it is an option. 
8. Use the app on your phone!
9. If you want to give this to a friend or a parent or a sibling, all they have to do is step 7.
